## Additional correctness seam accepted by SOL

A sanitized checkpoint is not a prefix of the raw delta stream. Source `content-processor.ts:132-149` trims/collapses whitespace and redacts patterns. Raw prefix `Hello ` becomes stored `Hello`; appending raw suffix `world` after reconnect gives `Helloworld`. A split email can similarly escape the intended provisional replacement behavior. Sequence numbers alone cannot repair a transformed prefix.

Accepted minimal rule: the original direct attachment may receive raw deltas; after any sanitized resnapshot, that attachment switches one-way to cumulative sanitized snapshot replacements until final. Never append raw suffixes to a sanitized base. No raw durable log, character-offset mapping or generic replay engine. Allow at most one coalesced cumulative snapshot of at most 1 MiB in addition to the 64 KiB delta queue; detach a stalled subscriber rather than queueing snapshots. Exercise reconnect at whitespace and redaction boundaries in real streaming acceptance. SOL explicitly accepted this seam in direct discussion; `response.md` records the complete final contract and remaining lead decisions.
